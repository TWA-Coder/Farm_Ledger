# Agricultural Insurance in Rwanda — Short Questionnaire

## Google Forms title and description

**Title:** Agricultural Insurance in Rwanda: A Short Survey

**Description:**

We want to understand people’s experiences and views about crop and livestock insurance in Rwanda. Anyone may respond, whether or not they have worked in farming or insurance. If you do not know an answer, choose “Not sure.”

Taking part is voluntary. You may stop at any time. Answers will be summarized for research and will not affect access to services. Please do not include names or other identifying information. We will not ask for your name or contact details. This survey takes about 3–5 minutes.

## Section 1 — Consent

### Q1. Do you agree to take part in this survey?
- **Google Forms type:** Multiple choice
- **Options:** Yes, I agree; No, I do not agree
- **Required:** Yes
- **Branching:** “Yes” → Section 2. “No” → Submit form.
- **Research purpose:** Confirms voluntary consent.

- **Branching:** None. Treat “I am not involved” and “Prefer not to say” as exclusive options.

## Section 4 — A possible digital approach

**Section description:**

Some digital systems can follow rules agreed in advance and take an action when reliable information confirms that the rules are met. For example, they might start a crop-insurance payment when trusted rainfall information confirms an agreed drought threshold, or move a livestock claim forward after veterinary verification. This depends on accurate information and clear rules. Some claims need human review. Blockchain is one possible way to keep digital records, but it does not by itself make information accurate or decisions fair.

### Q8. Based on this description, how comfortable would you be with some insurance steps being handled automatically?
- **Google Forms type:** Multiple choice
- **Options:** I would be comfortable; I might be comfortable; I am not sure; I would be uncomfortable; I need more information
- **Required:** Yes
- **Branching:** None.
- **Research purpose:** Measures acceptance without assuming that automation or blockchain is desirable.

### Q9. What would matter most for such a system to be trustworthy and useful? Select up to 3.
- **Google Forms type:** Checkboxes
- **Options:** Faster payments; Clear information about decisions; Reliable information; Human review and appeals; Protection of personal information; Affordable costs; Easy to use without a smartphone or internet; Fair treatment; I would not use such a system; Not sure; Other
- **Required:** Yes
- **Branching:** None. Set a maximum of 3 selections. Treat “I would not use such a system” and “Not sure” as exclusive options.
- **Research purpose:** Identifies priority benefits and safeguards, including fairness, accessibility, privacy, and human oversight.

## Section 5 — Final thoughts

### Q10. What one change would most improve crop or livestock insurance for people in Rwanda?
